# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository: open the "Security" tab and choose "Report a vulnerability". Do not open a public issue for a security problem.

## What to include

- What the problem is and what an attacker could do with it.
- The steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept.
- The affected part of the project (the API, the worker, the phone app, the website or the data pipeline) and, if you know it, the commit or version.
- Whether the problem is already known to anyone else.

Please do not access, change or delete other people's data while testing, and give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it.

## What to expect

The project has one maintainer, and reports are answered as the maintainer can. You will hear back once the report has been read, and again when a fix is ready.
