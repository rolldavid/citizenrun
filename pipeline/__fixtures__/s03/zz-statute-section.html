ZZ Fixture Code 24.050

Filing period: declarations of candidacy.

Fixture text: except as provided in this section, declarations of candidacy for the offices that are scheduled to be voted upon for the primary shall be filed during regular business hours with the filing officer no earlier than the first Monday in May and no later than the following Friday in the year in which the office is scheduled to be voted upon.

Fixture text: the filing period is a week, beginning the first Monday in May and ending the following Friday, and no declaration of candidacy received after the close of the period is accepted.
