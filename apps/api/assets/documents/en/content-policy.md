## What is welcome

Hard criticism, satire and strong language about candidates, officials and their records stay up. Debate about public life is the point of {{appName}}. Plain profanity is never a reason to hold or remove a post. Post about the offices on your ballot, the people who hold them and why you might run.

## What we remove

### Harassment

Targeting a person with repeated or severe abuse meant to intimidate or silence them. Example: sending a member a stream of insulting comments after they asked you to stop.

### Threats

Saying or implying that you or someone else will harm a person or their property. Example: writing that a candidate should watch out on the way home.

### Doxxing

Sharing someone's private information, such as a home address, a personal phone number or a workplace, without their consent. Example: posting a photo of an official's house with its street address.

### Impersonation

Pretending to be another person, a candidate, an official or an organization. Example: an account using a mayor's name and photo to post statements the mayor never made.

### Sexual content

Nudity or sexual activity. Example: a sexually explicit photo posted to a comment thread.

### Violent content

Graphic violence or gore, or praise for violent acts. Example: a video of a real attack posted to cheer it on.

### False claims about how, when or where to vote

Wrong information about how, when or where to vote, or about who may vote. Example: a post saying that voters of one party vote on a different day.

### Manipulated media of a real candidate or official

Images, audio or video of a real candidate or official that were made or changed, including with AI, and are presented as real. Example: a made-up recording of a candidate admitting to a crime.

### Intimate images shared without consent

Nude or sexual images of a real person shared without their consent. Example: a former partner's private photo posted to embarrass them.

### Child sexual abuse and exploitation

Any content that sexualizes a minor or puts children at risk of abuse. Example: any sexual image of a person under 18.

### Spam

Repeated, unwanted or misleading posts, links or messages. Example: the same promotional link pasted into dozens of comment threads.

### Copyright infringement

Posting someone else's work without permission where the law does not allow it. Example: uploading a news photographer's photo you have no right to share.

## Child safety

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. We remove it, close the accounts involved and report it to NCMEC, as the law requires. To report it, use the report button in the app or write to the support address below.

## Intimate images

We remove intimate images shared without consent within 48 hours of a valid request. To ask, report the image in the app or write to the support address below, as the [terms of use](/terms) explain.

## Impersonation and badges

Only a candidate who has claimed their page and been confirmed carries the badge. Anyone else using a candidate's or official's name must make clear that they are not that person.

## Elections

False information about how, when or where to vote, and manipulated media of real candidates and officials, are removed. Claims about the outcome of an election are not removed for that reason alone.

## How enforcement works

- A keyword filter holds some words and phrases for review before they are shown.
- Uploaded images are screened automatically for nudity, sexual content and gore.
- Public posts, comments and bios that our filters flag may be screened with AI by Anthropic. Direct messages are never screened with AI.
- When three different people report a post or comment, it is hidden until an admin reviews it.
- An admin reviews reports, and we aim to decide on every report within 24 hours.
- The person who reported and the author are told the outcome.
- You can appeal a decision by email to the support address below.

## New accounts

For the first 7 days, a new account may make 5 posts, 30 comments and 100 follows a day, and may not post links. After that, every account may make 20 posts and 200 comments a day.

## Messages

A message request from someone you do not follow waits until you accept it. You can block anyone who messages you and report a conversation from the app.
